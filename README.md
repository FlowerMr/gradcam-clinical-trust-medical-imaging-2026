
# Enhancing Clinical Trust in Deep Learning Models Through Grad-CAM-Based Explainability in Radiological Image Classification

## Overview

This repository contains the research paper presented at the **First National Conference of the Iranian Artificial Intelligence Association**, held at Sirjan University of Technology, Iran, on October 29–30, 2026.

The research explores the role of Grad-CAM-based explainability in improving the interpretability and clinical trustworthiness of deep learning models for radiological image classification.

## Research Information

- **Author:** Mohammadreza Golkar Khouzani
- **Research Area:** Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI)
- **Domain:** Medical Imaging
- **Method:** Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM)
- **Conference:** First National Conference of the Iranian Artificial Intelligence Association
